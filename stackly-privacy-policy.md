## 3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

### A. Local Device Storage (Non-Personal Gameplay Data)
Stackly stores essential gameplay progress and user preferences locally on your physical device storage (via secure LocalStorage):
- High scores, active score tallies, and combo multipliers.
- Visual custom preferences (e.g., Cyber Neon / Pastel themes, zoom factor, bloom glow, slice physics).
- Sound effects and music mute toggles.
- Premium ad-free status flags.

*Note: This gameplay data remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded, transferred, or sold to external servers.*

### B. Device Telemetry & Fleet Administration (Supabase)
To ensure server uptime, distribute emergency maintenance notices, and safeguard application integrity, Stackly performs an automated, encrypted handshake with our secure cloud infrastructure (**Supabase**):
- **Pseudonymous Anonymous Tag:** A generic, non-personal player label (e.g., `Stacker #6611`) generated randomly on initial launch.
- **Hardware Profile:** Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, and installed build version (`1.0.0`).
- **Session Telemetry:** Total lifetime session count and timestamp of recent activity to ensure minimum required version compliance.
- **Device Identifier:** A randomly generated alphanumeric hardware UUID (`dev_...`) used exclusively for administration, such as enforcing maintenance lockouts or account suspensions.

### C. Advertising Networks (Google AdMob)
Stackly is supported by advertising delivered through the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to allow free access to the game for all players:
- **App Open Ads:** Full-screen advertisements displayed upon application cold launch or resuming from the background.
- **Banner Ads:** Persistent banner advertisements anchored to the bottom of the display.
- **Rewarded Video Ads:** Optional, user-initiated video advertisements that reward players with in-game tower revives.

Google AdMob may collect and process your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), coarse location derived from IP address, and ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, completed views). For more information on how Google processes ad data, please review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. Data Encryption and Transmission Security

We prioritize the security of your data:
- **Encryption in Transit:** 100% of network requests between Stackly, Google AdMob, and our backend servers are encrypted using modern **Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS)** protocols.
- **No Third-Party Brokers:** We never sell, rent, or trade any user information to third-party data brokers.

## 5. Account Creation and Data Deletion Policy

- **No User Account Requirement:** Stackly does not require registration, login credentials, passwords, or personal email addresses to play.
- **Deleting Local Data:** You can delete all locally cached game data at any time by navigating to your device's **Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Stackly ➔ Clear Data / Storage**, or by uninstalling the app.
- **Resetting Ad Identifiers:** You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via **Android Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Delete Advertising ID**.
- **Requesting Telemetry Removal:** If you wish to have your pseudonymous telemetry entry removed from our backend, you may contact our developer support team at `allgames9e@gmail.com` with your player tag.
